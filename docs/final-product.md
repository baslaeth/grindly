## Product decisions

- Visitors see a short Grindly description, one Get started action and a six-step member journey. Entry leads to the existing invitation/email/wallet/testnet-NFT flow, not an invented checkout.
- Signed-in members with active research access get Open your Hub, Submit alpha, Following and updates and My contributions. The ecosystem explanation is available in a native keyboard-operable disclosure; recent alphas remain immediately useful.
- Nine named specialties share a same-rank collaboration area. Room links come only from the permission-filtered snapshot. Visitors are directed to Join. The five existing rank badges remain authoritative visual markers.
- NFT access and reviewed contribution history are available; upgrades, XP conversion, token claims, delegation, earning features and premium specialist groups are explicitly not active. No access to lower ranks is implied.
- Intelligence leads with saved analysis highlights and uncertainty before next action, evidence-stage metadata and detailed source/model/reviewer material. Saved timestamps, experimental limitations, historical labels and all underlying claims/quotations remain accessible. Retrieved pages do not establish claim truth.
- Forms use side-by-side category/type controls on desktop, single-line subject/URL/identifier inputs, and multi-line reasoning/steps. Unknown and Not applicable remain supported; existing payloads and validation are preserved. Longer category answers now have usable text areas.
