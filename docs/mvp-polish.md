## Boundaries

No schema replay, identity changes, new reward rules, claims or upgrades. Existing
demo identities only for test activity; never represent samples as member adoption.
Public Vercel cannot reach the loopback Ollama service. No tunnel, paid model,
unapproved provider or externally exposed local credential will be introduced.
Saved local results may be inspected publicly by permitted members, with their
original model and timestamps. Fresh source checks are not fresh model analysis.
