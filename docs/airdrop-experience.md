## Repeatable browser route

Review website: http://localhost:3000/ . Localhost is available only while its
server runs on this computer; it is not a 24-hour hosted preview.

1. Home -> Airdrop Hunters -> Submit alpha. Choose Airdrop Hunters and Guide.
   Supply project, network, official document, confirmed facts and short steps.
   Keep speculation, prerequisites and execution evidence distinct. Explicit
   Unknown is accepted; do not invent a successful test or reward.
2. Save -> Alpha saved. The receipt retains its date even if review later changes.
   Same-rank sharing is pending evaluation, not approval.
3. Inspect evidence in Grind Intelligence. Open source checks, refresh, inspect
   missing information and optional local model evidence. A source/model failure
   does not erase the saved version. Exact quotes do not prove model reasoning.
4. A separately scoped reviewer opens Review Desk. The author cannot self-approve.
   A sourced decision and any actual XP appear in the author's Activity/Profile.
5. Follow on the alpha. Home -> Following and updates -> Airdrop alerts. Choose
   important alert types. Supported configured sources show last successful check;
   unsupported sources must not be treated as monitored.
6. Confirmed updates show the event, required action, separate dates and source.
   Acknowledge/Done retain history. My Profile remains top-right, with progression
   and the disabled Claim $GRIND control. Home/Profile link to `/xp`.
